# Aily Labs

> Aily Labs is a Munich-based enterprise AI company that builds an AI Decision Intelligence Suite — a platform connecting siloed internal and external data to deliver real-time, explainable predictions and recommendations across enterprise functions. Aily Labs is a portfolio company of Insight Partners. It exposes no public developer API; this profile captures its corporate, security, and compliance surface.

## About
- Product: AI Decision Intelligence Suite (300+ validated predictive models, 1-day deployment, iOS app)
- Functions covered: Finance, R&D, Supply Chain, Manufacturing, Quality, Go-To-Market, Procurement, People
- Industries: Healthcare & Life Sciences, Consumer Goods, Luxury & Fashion, Media & Tech, Retail, Services
- Notable client: Sanofi
- Backed by: Insight Partners
